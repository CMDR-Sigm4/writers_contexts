## ACTIVE ITEMS

### Old Kitchen Cleaver
**Current holder:** Liang Wei
**First appearance:** Cap. 1

**[Cap. 1]** Traditional, heavy kitchen cleaver with a dark worn handle. Liang carries it while working and sets it near the chopping block. Its presence makes him look harsher than his small acts of kindness suggest.

### Brass Lighter
**Current holder:** Liang Wei
**First appearance:** Cap. 1

**[Cap. 1]** Scratched brass lighter used when Liang steps into the alley to smoke. It clicks before the cigarette glow appears, becoming one of the small sounds the cats recognize.

### Main Alley Trash Bin
**Current holder:** Chinatown Alley
**First appearance:** Cap. 1

**[Cap. 1]** Dented metal trash bin behind the restaurant. It smells of fish, old rice, and frying oil. Daphne treats it as territory; Milo contests it through proximity rather than violence.

### Scrap Bowl
**Current holder:** Golden Carp Kitchen
**First appearance:** Cap. 1

**[Cap. 1]** Small bowl of edible scraps Liang pretends are only kitchen waste. He occasionally gives pieces to the cats.

### Liang's Dirty Apron
**Current holder:** Liang Wei
**First appearance:** Cap. 1

**[Cap. 1]** White kitchen apron stained with oil, soy, fish scales, and smoke. It marks Liang as part of the kitchen's labor and makes his kindness look rougher, almost accidental. Visual reference reinforces the apron as central to his silhouette.

### Cigarette Pack
**Current holder:** Liang Wei
**First appearance:** Cap. 1

**[Cap. 1]** Liang keeps cigarettes close during service and steps into the alley to smoke after trash runs. The cigarette may wait behind his ear or hang from his mouth, making smoke part of his recognizable presence.

### Tall Cook's Hat
**Current holder:** Liang Wei
**First appearance:** Cap. 1

**[Cap. 1]** Tall white cook's hat from the visual reference. It gives Liang a sharper, more recognizable kitchen silhouette: formal in shape, worn down by the tired practicality of the Golden Carp's back-of-house.

### Sleeveless Kitchen Undershirt
**Current holder:** Liang Wei
**First appearance:** Cap. 1

**[Cap. 1]** Practical undershirt or thin kitchen layer beneath Liang's apron, inspired by the visual reference. It emphasizes heat, labor, and the exposed, unpolished physicality of kitchen work.

### Broken Crate
**Current holder:** Chinatown Alley
**First appearance:** Cap. 1

**[Cap. 1]** Broken crate near the alley wall. Milo emerges from under it, making it one of his hiding places and possible lookout points.

### Fish Spine
**Current holder:** Milo
**First appearance:** Cap. 1

**[Cap. 1]** Silver fish spine from the trash bin. Milo takes it only after Daphne's small movement grants permission, making it a token of tolerated access rather than victory.

### Trash Bag
**Current holder:** Chinatown Alley
**First appearance:** Cap. 1

**[Cap. 1]** Heavy kitchen trash bag carried out by Liang after service. It marks the transition from kitchen scene to alley scene and brings the strongest food smells into animal territory.

### Greasy Kitchen Window
**Current holder:** Golden Carp Restaurant
**First appearance:** Cap. 1

**[Cap. 1]** Back kitchen window leaking red restaurant light into the alley. It visually binds Daphne to the restaurant territory and gives the alley its smoky red nighttime cast.

### Chopping Block
**Current holder:** Golden Carp Kitchen
**First appearance:** Cap. 1

**[Cap. 1]** Worn kitchen block where Liang's cleaver strikes scallions and fish joints. It anchors the kitchen soundscape and the repeated metal-on-wood rhythm the animals hear outside.

### Dented Pot
**Current holder:** Golden Carp Kitchen
**First appearance:** Cap. 1

**[Cap. 1]** Dented pot partly hiding the scrap bowl near the back counter. It helps conceal the unofficial route by which edible waste becomes food for the alley animals.
