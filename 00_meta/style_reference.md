# Style Reference

**Instructions for the AI Agent:**
Before generating prose for `working_draft.md`, read this file to calibrate style, vocabulary, and rhythm.

## Core Style Rules — The Bin

1. **Small stakes, precise pressure.** Food, warmth, rank, and habit matter. Treat them with the seriousness another story might give to war.
2. **Animal perception first.** For cat-centered scenes, lead with smell, temperature, distance, sound, posture, and movement. Avoid abstract human psychology unless grounded in behavior.
3. **No cute animal speech.** The cats do not talk. Their character appears through timing, gaze, tail, ears, paws, stillness, retreat, and appetite.
4. **Silence carries rank.** Daphne's authority should often be shown by what she does not need to do. No hissing unless a boundary truly breaks.
5. **Milo is kinetic.** He advances, remembers caution too late, converts mistakes into play, and survives because others decide not to punish him fully.
6. **Liang's kindness is disguised.** He complains, points, mutters, smokes, and still drops food where a cat can reach it.
7. **Material vocabulary.** Fish bones, old rice, frying oil, wet cardboard, brass, cleaver steel, cigarette ash, steam, red kitchen light, concrete, grease.
8. **Modern nocturnal Chinatown.** Neon, deliveries, late kitchens, vents, scooters, damp asphalt, stacked crates, shuttered shops, and the private life behind public storefronts.
9. **Dialogue is sparse.** Human lines should be short, physical, and often funny without breaking the mood.
10. **Endings stay open.** Close chapters on a gesture, a withheld reaction, a small permission, or a silence that implies tomorrow.

## Rhythm

- Use short paragraphs for pauses and social pressure.
- Let repeated concrete sounds structure the scene: cleaver strikes, lighter clicks, vent hum, bin lid shift.
- Avoid explaining what the reader can infer from movement.

## Style Sample to Imitate

> The bin belongs to Daphne before Milo reaches it.
>
> Not by law. Not by teeth. Not tonight.
>
> The alley behind the Golden Carp is wet enough to remember rain, though the sky has cleared and the city has gone hard and black above the roofs. Neon trembles in the puddles. Steam crawls out of a vent near the kitchen door and spreads through the smoke already hanging there, thick with fish, garlic, frying oil, and the sour rot of cabbage gone soft in plastic bags.
>
> Daphne sits beside the main trash bin as if someone placed her there with purpose.
>
> Her paws are close together. Her short blue-gray coat takes the red leaking from the kitchen window and turns it to steel. Her green eyes hold the alley without hurry. Nothing else in her moves.
>
> Milo comes from under the broken crate.
>
> He comes too quickly at first, then remembers himself halfway across the concrete and slows. His tail lifts. Drops. Lifts again.
