# Golden Carp Restaurant

## Core Concept

The Golden Carp is a small present-day Chinatown restaurant with a public dining room, a hard-working kitchen, and a service alley that supports a second, unofficial ecosystem.

## Human Order

- **Mei-Lin Chen:** owner; controls money, reputation, schedules, and customer-facing discipline.
- **Liang Wei:** cook; controls the practical heat of the kitchen: knives, timing, scraps, smoke breaks.

## Back-of-House Culture

The kitchen is loud, hot, and efficient. Waste is watched, but some edible scraps quietly become offerings to alley animals. No one fully admits this system exists.

## Relationship to the Alley

The restaurant produces the smells, heat, leftovers, and routines that shape the alley's animal society. The back door is a border: humans cross it to smoke or dump trash; animals cross it only through timing and tolerated trespass.

## Sensory Markers

Garlic, ginger, fish, frying oil, wet cardboard, cigarette smoke, metal, steam, red kitchen light.

## Open Questions

- Is the Golden Carp struggling financially or merely exhausted by ordinary survival?
- Does Mei-Lin secretly allow the animal feeding because it keeps pests away?
