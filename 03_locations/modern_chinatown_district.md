# Modern Chinatown District

## General Atmosphere

A present-day urban Chinatown district: dense storefronts, late kitchens, delivery scooters, neon signs, old family businesses, and tourists passing through without seeing the service alleys behind the light.

## Sight

Glass, steam, signage, wet asphalt, stacked crates, bright restaurant windows, and shadowed alleys behind them.

## Sound

Traffic, delivery brakes, kitchen fans, overlapping languages, late laughter, metal shutters, and the low hum of refrigeration.

## Smell

Roast meat, incense from a nearby shop, fish markets closing down, rain, exhaust, cigarette smoke.

## Narrative Notes

The public street is bright and transactional. The alley is older, more honest, and less forgiving.
