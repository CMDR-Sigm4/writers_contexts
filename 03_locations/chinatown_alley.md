# Chinatown Alley

## General Atmosphere

A narrow present-day Chinatown alley behind a small restaurant. At night it is smoky, wet, and crowded with smells: fish, old oil, cigarette ash, spoiled cabbage, and rain trapped in concrete seams.

## Sight

- Metal trash bins with dented lids.
- Flattened cardboard near the back door.
- Red restaurant light leaking through a greasy kitchen window.
- Steam from vents and cigarette smoke folding together.
- A narrow strip of neon reflected in puddles.

## Sound

- Kitchen metal: cleavers, pans, ladles.
- A refrigerator motor behind the wall.
- Soft animal movement under trash bins.
- Distant traffic and late-night voices from the street.

## Smell

Fish bones, old rice, frying oil, wet cardboard, garlic, ginger, bleach, tobacco smoke.

## Touch / Temperature

Cold damp concrete. Warmth near the kitchen door. Grease-slick metal handles. The air feels thick in the throat.

## Points of Interest

- Main trash bin: contested feeding ground.
- Back kitchen door.
- Vent where Old Bao sleeps.
- Broken crate near the wall, useful as a lookout for cats.

## Narrative Notes

The alley has its own social order. Daphne controls access by presence rather than violence. Humans think of it as service space; animals know it as territory.
