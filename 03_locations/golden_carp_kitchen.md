# Golden Carp Kitchen

## General Atmosphere

The working kitchen of the restaurant: hot, loud, crowded, and bright in a way that makes every shadow look greasy.

## Sight

- Stainless counters scratched by years of chopping.
- Hanging ladles, wire baskets, and blackened pans.
- A chopping block darkened by soy, fish blood, and age.
- Liang's dirty apron moving between steam and flame.

## Sound

Cleaver strikes, wok hiss, burners coughing blue fire, the owner's voice cutting through orders, water running too long in a metal sink.

## Smell

Ginger, garlic, fish, hot oil, smoke, old wood, metal, and the sour edge of trash waiting to be taken out.

## Touch / Temperature

Heat around the stoves. Damp cold near the back door. Slick floor tiles. Sharp metal always too close to the hand.

## Points of Interest

- Chopping block.
- Back door to the alley.
- Scrap bowl that Liang pretends is only waste.
- Hook where the old cleaver hangs.

## Narrative Notes

The kitchen is human territory, but animals enter it through habit, timing, and Liang's inconsistent mercy.
