# Master Outline

## Working Title

The Bin

## Logline

A nocturnal micro-drama unfolds behind a Chinatown restaurant, where alley cats, an old dog, and exhausted kitchen workers negotiate territory, hunger, rank, and quiet mercy without ever naming it.

## Premise

The Golden Carp restaurant has two worlds: the bright, hot, human world of the kitchen, and the damp service alley behind it. The kitchen runs on orders, knives, money, and exhaustion. The alley runs on scent, timing, seniority, and the unspoken law of who moves first.

Daphne, an adult female cat, controls the main trash bin not through violence but through reputation. Milo, young and almost naive, keeps testing the border between permission and punishment. Liang Wei, the cook, belongs to both worlds: cleaver and apron inside, cigarette and scraps outside. Mei-Lin Chen owns the restaurant and its human order. Old Bao, the dog by the vent, confirms the animal order by respecting Daphne.

## Core Themes

- Authority without spectacle.
- Hunger as social pressure.
- Kindness hidden behind irritation.
- Territory as memory and habit.
- The parallel lives of humans and animals in the same urban space.
