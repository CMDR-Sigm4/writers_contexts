# Mei-Lin Chen

## Role

Owner of the restaurant. Practical, sharp-eyed, and protective of the business reputation.

## Appearance

- Adult woman with neat hair and a controlled, efficient presence.
- Dresses cleanly even during late service.
- Carries keys, receipts, and the small fatigue of someone who never fully stops working.

## Voice and Dialogue Habits

Precise, clipped, and managerial. She notices waste, noise, lateness, and anything that might trouble customers.

## Motivation

Keep the restaurant alive, orderly, profitable, and respected.

## Fatal Flaw

She can value control so highly that she misses quiet loyalties forming around her.

## Relationship Snapshot

- **Liang:** relies on his skill and tolerates his habits more than she admits.
- **Daphne and Milo:** knows the cats exist; treats them as a nuisance unless they draw attention.
- **Old Bao:** tolerated because he keeps rats away and belongs to the rhythm of the alley.

## Canon Notes

Mei-Lin does not appear in the test chapter directly except as a pressure inside the kitchen: Liang knows what she would complain about.

## Dialogue Sample

"Liang. Smoke after the trash, not before."

## Open Questions

- Whether she inherited the restaurant or built it herself.
- Whether she secretly permits Liang's scraps for the cats.
