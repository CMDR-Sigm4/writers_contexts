# Old Bao

## Role

Older dog who lingers near the restaurant alley. Not owned by the cats, not their enemy, and not foolish enough to challenge Daphne.

## Appearance

- Large, aging mixed-breed dog.
- Heavy muzzle, clouded eyes, and scarred ears.
- Sleeps near warm vents or cardboard when the alley is quiet.

## Voice and Dialogue Habits

Old Bao communicates through weight, breath, and refusal to move unless movement matters.

## Motivation

Warmth, scraps, routine, and peace.

## Fatal Flaw

He is slow to respond until a boundary has already been crossed.

## Relationship Snapshot

- **Daphne:** respects her seniority inside the alley's animal order.
- **Milo:** ignores him unless Milo becomes too foolish.
- **Liang:** accepts thrown scraps without gratitude or complaint.

## Canon Notes

Old Bao's deference to Daphne establishes her authority. He may be physically larger, but the alley does not belong to size alone.

## Open Questions

- Whether he once belonged to someone connected to the restaurant.
