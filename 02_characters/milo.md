# Milo

## Role

Young male alley cat. Curious, playful, and almost naive. He is bold mostly because he has not yet learned the full cost of being bold.

## Appearance

- Lean, still growing into his paws.
- Short fur with dark ginger and white patches.
- Bright eyes, usually too open for a street cat.
- A nick on one ear that he treats like a medal.

## Voice and Dialogue Habits

Milo does not speak in human language in prose. His "voice" is physical: sudden steps, tail flicks, tilted head, soft paw taps, and the audacity of getting too close.

If rendered in internal animal logic, keep him simple, immediate, and sensory.

## Motivation

Food, warmth, play, and the desire to be seen by the older animals without being swatted away.

## Fatal Flaw

He mistakes tolerance for permission.

## Relationship Snapshot

- **Daphne:** admires and fears her. Wants access to what she controls.
- **Liang:** associates him with smoke, meat scraps, and unpredictable kindness.
- **Old Bao:** respects him without fully understanding why.

## Canon Notes

Milo is not aggressive. Even when contesting food, he uses approach, retreat, posture, and timing rather than open attack.

## Dialogue Sample

Not applicable. Use body language instead of speech.

## Open Questions

- Whether Milo belongs to anyone or was born in the alley.
- Whether his naivety will harden or remain part of his charm.
