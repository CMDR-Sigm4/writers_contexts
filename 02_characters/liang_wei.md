# Liang Wei

## Role

Chinese cook at the restaurant bordering the alley. Rough-handed, overworked, and more sentimental than he admits.

## Appearance

- Older than first impression: late middle-aged to elderly, wiry, and smoke-stained.
- Narrow, tired profile with a lined face and the look of someone listening to kitchen noise even during pauses.
- Often wears a tall white cook's hat, slightly too formal for the worn back-of-house around him.
- Wears a dirty white apron marked with oil, soy, and fish scales, often over a sleeveless undershirt or thin kitchen shirt.
- Often carries an old kitchen cleaver with a dark handle.
- Fingers yellowed by cigarettes.
- Cigarette often hangs from his mouth or waits behind one ear, depending on whether Mei-Lin is watching.

## Posture and Movement

- Works from the side of his body: shoulder turned, head angled, eyes cutting toward trouble before his feet move.
- Resting posture looks like a pause stolen from labor rather than real rest.
- Holds still with smoke in his mouth, then moves abruptly when the kitchen demands it.

## Voice and Dialogue Habits

Liang speaks fluent English, but with a noticeable Chinese accent, compact rhythm, and occasional words from his own language when irritated, amused, or tired. His meaning is always clear; the accent should shape cadence, not reduce intelligence or turn him into caricature.

He speaks in short, amused fragments when outside with the animals. He laughs under his breath more often than he explains himself. Use code-switching sparingly: a muttered "aiya," a term of address, or a small curse can surface when the kitchen hurts him or the cats test his patience.

Example: "Aiya. Still alive, little thief?"

## Motivation

Keep the kitchen moving, keep the owner from shouting too long, and feed the cats just enough to pretend he is not feeding them.

## Fatal Flaw

He hides kindness behind irritation until it becomes difficult to tell the two apart.

## Relationship Snapshot

- **Daphne:** respects her distance and sometimes leaves the better scraps where she can reach them first.
- **Milo:** teases him, feeds him, and expects him to be foolish.
- **Mei-Lin Chen:** works under her authority; grumbles but obeys.
- **Old Bao:** treats him like part of the alley furniture.

## Canon Notes

Liang is a hardened smoker. He steps into the alley to smoke after kitchen rushes and often carries food scraps or trash with him.
