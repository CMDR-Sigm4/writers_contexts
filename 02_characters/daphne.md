# Daphne

## Role

Adult female alley cat. Territorial, composed, and respected by the other animals around the restaurant, including the dog that sleeps near the back door.

## Appearance

- Compact, strong body with controlled movements.
- Short blue-gray coat, dense and sleek, catching alley light as steel rather than softness.
- Bright green eyes that stay fixed longer than anyone else's.
- Large upright ears, alert without fidgeting.
- A composed face that makes stillness feel like a decision.
